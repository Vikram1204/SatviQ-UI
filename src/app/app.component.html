Gestation & PD Timeline

Standard clinical lifecycle for bovine reproduction.

Day 1

Breeding / AI Event

Artificial Insemination recorded with semen batch details and veterinarian verification.

Day 30 - 45

Pregnancy Diagnosis (PD)

Ultrasonography or rectal palpation performed to confirm embryonic presence.

Day 90

Secondary PD Confirmation

Recheck to confirm pregnancy stability and monitor embryonic development.

Day 220

Dry Off Period

Milk production is stopped to allow mammary gland recovery ahead of calving.

Day 283

Calving & Lactation Reset

Expected delivery of calf. Cow enters new lactation cycle and milking resumes.
